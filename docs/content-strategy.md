> 광림전산은 전산용품, 사무용품, 인쇄물, 판촉물, 산업용 잡자재까지 필요한 품목을 확인하고 빠르게 공급합니다. 대량 납품부터 소량 제작 문의까지 편하게 상담하세요.

## Tone

- Clear
- Practical
- Trustworthy
- Local
- Direct

Avoid vague phrases such as "best solution", "future innovation", or "premium experience" unless supported by concrete content.

## Customer Message Rules

Public institutions:

- Emphasize quote, delivery, office supplies, printed materials, and event goods.

Companies and factories:

- Emphasize regular supply, consumables, computer peripherals, and industrial supplies.

Individual customers:

- Emphasize easy inquiry, small quantities, business cards, stickers, stamps, banners, and accessories.
